RAL's LTGG offers 3× system mass reduction over the existing pneumatic system. This project designed and manufactured LTGG ground test hardware for a 90 L toroidal bag, executed the 48-hour validation campaign at VSSC, and secured approval for flight hardware development.

My Role: Complete design and manufacture of LTGG hardware; sole execution of system validation at VSSC; data analysis for condensation correction, leak integrity, and NRV/PRV verification.
Key Contributions

  • Conducted 48-hour system validation at VSSC — confirmed sustained pressure, structural integrity, and leak tightness
  • Characterised condensation-induced pressure drop, isolating it from genuine leakage via temperature logging and thermodynamic analysis
  • Verified NRV and PRV operation under representative conditions — one-way flow control and overpressure protection confirmed

Overview

DRDO NSDL required a gas generation system to inflate 160 L and 520 L toroidal recovery bags for torpedo retrieval. Unlike prior LTGG applications at 0.2 bar, this system demanded 5–10 bar differential pressure — requiring distinct chamber design, 50 bar proof plumbing, and thermal management to prevent bag material damage.

Rapid Failure Investigation: Temperature overshoot identified during initial testing. Root cause — insufficient thermal damping in gas path. Corrective heat-soak insert designed, manufactured, and validated within 2 days.
Key Contributions
  • Designed LTGG combustion chambers for 160 L and 520 L at 5–10 bar Pd — adapting charge sizing and orifice geometry from low-pressure designs
  • Designed all plumbing hardware from scratch: pipes, connectors, seals, O-rings, manifolds — rated to 50 bar proof, ~1 kg/s mass flow
  • Designed multi-channel instrumentation system (300–1000 Hz) capturing Pressure vs Time performance curves
  • Executed full system-level test matrix at NSDL-DRDO characterising both configurations
  • Performed thermal root cause analysis — designed and manufactured corrective insert within 2-day turnaround; retested and verified
Challenges & Solutions
⚠ ChallengeScaling from 0.2 bar to 5–10 bar Pd: combustion chamber design, increased FoS for Ground test, 50 bar proof plumbing
✓ SolutionInnovative combustion chamber geometry design based on L/D (available volume), propellant charge, and orifice geometry; conservative margins on all pressure-boundary components
⚠ ChallengeTemperature overshoot at bag inlet — risk of bag material degradation, identified during NSDL testing
✓ SolutionThermal insert added to plumbing to increase convective heat transfer; designed, manufactured, and retested within 2 days
Outcomes
Both configs validated at NSDL-DRDO TRL 2–3 → 7–8 in 5–7 months Temp overshoot resolved in 2 days Naval defence-ready design delivered

Overview

Hybrid rocket development programme at RAL targeting aluminized paraffin wax + GOX combustion at 60 bar chamber pressure. Tested at the ISRO SDSC sub-scale facility, Sriharikota — among very few hybrid systems globally to achieve stable combustion at this pressure. The 6-second test validated fuel grain quality, combustion stability, and jet deflector geometry.

My Role: Propellant preparation — aluminized paraffin wax fuel grain casting and resin-based thermal insulation casting. Supported test operations at SDSC facility.
Engineering Work Performed
  • Cast aluminized paraffin wax fuel grains — controlled pour temperature, fill sequence, and solidification to achieve void-free grain with near-zero blow-holes
  • Cast resin-based thermal insulation for fuel grain outer surface — protecting motor case from thermal loading during combustion
  • Supported pre-test system checks and safety procedures at SDSC sub-scale launch pad facility within ISRO's test site protocols
Challenges & Solutions
⚠ ChallengeBlow-holes in wax fuel grain at 60 bar create local burn-through and combustion instability — defects amplified at high pressure
✓ SolutionCareful pour temperature control, sequential section fill, and vacuum de-aeration → near-zero blow-hole rate achieved
⚠ ChallengeStable combustion at 60 bar — most hybrid systems operate at much lower Pc; instability risk scales with pressure
✓ SolutionCorrect oxidiser mass flux and grain port geometry — grain defects critical at high pressure, so casting quality paramount
Outcomes
Stable combustion at 60 bar validated ~1 kN thrust for 6 seconds Jet deflector geometry validated at SDSC Casting processes qualified

Spacecraft Propulsion Focus Areas

Academic and research interests being developed during MSc studies at Cranfield University.

🧪
Chemical Propulsion

Liquid and solid propellant systems, combustion instability, nozzle design, and high-performance propellant formulations for spacecraft and launch vehicle applications.

🌱
Green Propellants

Low-toxicity, high-performance monopropellant and bipropellant alternatives. Interest in AF-M315E, LMP-103S, and hydrogen peroxide-based propulsion systems.

Hybrid Propulsion

Ion thrusters, Hall-effect thrusters, and electrospray propulsion for small satellites and deep-space missions. High specific impulse, low-thrust manoeuvring.

Hydrogen Propulsion

Cryogenic hydrogen as a propellant for upper stages and future spacecraft. Slush hydrogen handling, boil-off management, and high-Isp cryogenic engine cycles.

Spacecraft Systems Engineering

Spacecraft propulsion system architecture, thruster selection and sizing, mission delta-V budgets, GNC interface, and propulsion subsystem integration.
